Privacy Policy

Terra Vista Winterization (Andy Witzke) · Effective September 26, 2026

This policy explains how Andy Witzke, who runs the Terra Vista Winterization sign-up program ("we"), handles your information.

Information we collect

  • Your name, mobile phone number and street address.
  • Any notes you add, such as gate codes or where your valve is.
  • The days and times that work for you, and whether you agreed to receive text messages.
  • On your service day: when your appointment was completed, and any notes from the technician.

How we use it

Only to run the winterization program: to schedule your appointment, plan the technician's route, and send you text messages about your appointment if you opted in.

Who can see it

Only Andy Witzke and the technician, Randy Kutter, see your information. It is also processed by the service providers that run the program (Twilio for text messages, Google Maps for address lookup, Railway for hosting), only to provide this service.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

Your information is deleted after the winterization season ends.

Your choices

  • Change or cancel your registration any time using the private link in your confirmation text.
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
  • To have your information deleted sooner, call or text Andy Witzke at 612-251-0478.

Contact

Andy Witzke, 612-251-0478.
