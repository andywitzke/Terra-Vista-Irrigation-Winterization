The program

Terra Vista Winterization is a sign-up and scheduling program for irrigation system winterization in the Terra Vista neighborhood, organized and run by Andy Witzke. Neighbors use this website to sign up, choose the days and times that work for them, and follow their appointment. The winterization work itself is performed by Randy Kutter, who is paid directly at the time of service.

Text messages (SMS)

  • Program name: Terra Vista Winterization (Andy Witzke).
  • What you'll receive: texts about your winterization appointment only: a sign-up confirmation with a link to manage your registration, a notice if your appointment date changes, "you're 2nd in line" and "you're next" alerts on your service day, a notice when your system has been winterized, and confirmation if you cancel or ask for your link. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.
  • How often: about 4 messages per appointment, up to 6 if your appointment changes.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Text messages are optional. You can sign up for winterization without agreeing to receive texts.
  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll get one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed, and no further texts will be sent.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call or text Andy Witzke at 612-251-0478.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
